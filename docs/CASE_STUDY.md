# 🤖 RightPlace AI

> Understanding people before matching opportunities.

---

# The Beginning

Like many professionals, I believed resumes were the problem.

People spent hours rewriting documents, adapting keywords, and trying to fit themselves into Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

The obvious solution seemed to be another AI-powered resume builder.

That was the original idea behind RightPlace AI.

But the deeper we explored the hiring process, the more we realized something important.

The resume was never the real problem.

It was only the final translation of a much bigger problem.

---

# The Real Problem

Traditional hiring systems evaluate documents.

People, however, are much more complex than documents.

A resume rarely explains:

- how someone solves problems;
- why they make certain decisions;
- what motivates them;
- how they learn;
- which skills can be transferred to another role;
- what they are actually capable of becoming.

Companies compare resumes.

Professionals try to optimize keywords.

Everyone loses information.

---

# The Insight

One idea completely changed the direction of the project.

**A resume is not a person.**

A resume is simply one possible translation of someone's professional story.

If we want better hiring decisions, we first need a better understanding of people.

That insight transformed RightPlace AI from a resume assistant into a Career Intelligence platform.

---

# Product Evolution

The product evolved through several iterations.

```
Resume Builder
      ↓
AI Resume Assistant
      ↓
Career Assistant
      ↓
Career Intelligence Platform
      ↓
Living Profiles
      ↓
North
```

Each iteration moved the focus away from documents and closer to people.

---

# The Living Profile

Instead of treating a resume as the source of truth, RightPlace AI builds what we call a Living Profile.

A Living Profile is continuously refined through conversations.

Every interaction adds context.

Every new experience improves understanding.

The profile evolves together with the professional instead of becoming obsolete after every career change.

The resume becomes only one possible output of that understanding.

---

# North

North is the conversational intelligence behind RightPlace AI.

Its role is not to write resumes.

Its role is to understand people.

North investigates professional experiences through evidence-based conversations, identifies transferable skills, recognizes patterns, and gradually builds a richer understanding of each professional.

Instead of asking:

> "What job have you done?"

North tries to answer:

> "What are you capable of becoming?"

---

# Design Principles

The product is guided by a few fundamental principles.

- People before Profiles.
- Conversation before Evaluation.
- Potential alongside Experience.
- Evidence before Assumptions.
- AI as an Assistant.
- Trust before Data.

These principles influence every product decision, from prompt engineering to interface design.

---

# Beyond Resumes

Although RightPlace AI can generate resumes, resumes are no longer the center of the product.

The same understanding can eventually support:

- professional matching;
- career recommendations;
- internal mobility;
- talent discovery;
- hiring support;
- long-term professional growth.

Understanding comes first.

Documents come later.
