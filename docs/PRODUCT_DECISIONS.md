# ⚖️ Product Decisions

> Every product is the result of thousands of decisions.
>
> This document explains the reasoning behind some of the most important design choices made during the development of RightPlace AI.

# Why conversations instead of forms?

Traditional career platforms rely on long forms.

People are asked to remember years of experience, summarize themselves into text fields, and rate their own abilities.

We chose conversations because stories reveal context.

Through dialogue, professionals naturally describe challenges, decisions, motivations and evidence that would rarely appear in a form.

The conversation becomes the discovery process.

# Why Living Profiles instead of static resumes?

A resume represents a moment.

A career does not.

Professionals learn, change industries, develop new skills and redefine their goals over time.

Instead of generating isolated resumes, RightPlace AI continuously builds a Living Profile that evolves alongside the professional.

Every conversation improves understanding.

# Why evidence instead of self-assessment?

People often struggle to evaluate themselves objectively.

Some underestimate their abilities.

Others overestimate them.

Rather than asking users to score themselves, North looks for evidence inside real experiences.

Decisions, actions, results and reflections are more reliable than confidence alone.

# Why transferable skills matter?

Many professionals have abilities that are hidden behind job titles.

A retail manager may have product management skills.

A military officer may have operations expertise.

A customer support specialist may demonstrate excellent UX research abilities.

North focuses on identifying capabilities that can move across industries rather than simply matching previous job titles.

# Why doesn't North flatter?

Trust is built through credibility.

Generic compliments create artificial conversations and reduce confidence in AI-generated insights.

North recognizes evidence.

It does not manufacture praise.

Recognition should always emerge from facts supported by the conversation.

# Why one question at a time?

Understanding requires focus.

Each question exists to reduce uncertainty, validate a hypothesis or discover missing context.

Long questionnaires create fatigue.

Short conversations create engagement.

# Why does North separate facts from hypotheses?

Professional decisions have consequences.

For that reason, North never presents assumptions as facts.

Every conclusion belongs to one of four categories:

- Facts
- Signals
- Hypotheses
- Unknowns

This distinction helps maintain transparency throughout the conversation.

# Why isn't the resume the product?

Most career platforms treat the resume as the final objective.

RightPlace AI does not.

The resume is simply one possible translation of the current understanding of a professional.

The understanding itself is far more valuable than any document generated from it.

# Why understand before matching?

Traditional hiring compares keywords.

RightPlace AI tries to understand people before comparing them.

Better understanding leads to better translation.

Better translation leads to better matching.

Matching is a consequence, not the starting point.

# Why can the conversation continue after generating a resume?

Generating a resume does not complete someone's professional journey.

New experiences create new evidence.

New evidence changes understanding.

Understanding improves the Living Profile.

For this reason, conversations never truly end.

Each interaction expands the professional profile instead of replacing it.

# Why AI as an assistant?

Artificial intelligence should support human decision-making, not replace it.

North helps organize information, identify patterns and translate professional experiences.

Hiring decisions, career choices and personal aspirations remain human responsibilities.

# A Principle Behind Every Decision

Every design choice in RightPlace AI can be traced back to a single idea.

> **People are not resumes.**
>
> **Resumes are translations.**
>
> **Understanding comes first.**

## Core Principles

- People before Profiles.
- Conversation before Evaluation.
- Potential alongside Experience.
- Evidence before Assumptions.
- AI as an Assistant.
- Understanding before Matching.
- Living Profiles instead of static resumes.
- Trust before Data.
